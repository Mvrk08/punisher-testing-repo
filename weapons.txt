# The Punisher's Weaponry Arsenal

Frank Castle utilizes a vast, pragmatic mix of military-grade firearms, explosives, and close-quarters tools.

## Handguns & Sidearms
* **Colt M1911A1:** Classic .45 ACP semi-automatic pistol; his primary choice.
* **Glock 19 / 26:** Reliable 9mm pistols favored for concealment and tactical backups.
* **Beretta 92FS:** Standard-issue sidearm frequently fitted with a suppressor.
* **SIG Sauer P226:** High-capacity duty pistol pulled from tactical resupplies.

## Rifles & Carbines
* **M4A1 Carbine:** Primary modular assault rifle, heavily modified with optics.
* **M16A2:** Traditional military rifle featured heavily in early comic runs.
* **HK416:** Premium tactical carbine used for high-threat operations.
* **AK-47:** Regularly scavenged from criminal cartels and enemy forces.

## Submachine Guns
* **HK MP5 / MP5K:** Standard 9mm close-quarters choice for room clearing.
* **Micro Uzi:** Ultra-compact weapon used for high-rate-of-fire spray.

## Shotguns
* **Remington 870:** Pump-action combat shotgun used for breaching and stopping power.
* **Mossberg 500 Cruiser:** Pistol-grip shotgun preferred for quick deployment.

## Heavy Firepower
* **M60 Machine Gun:** His iconic belt-fed weapon for heavy suppression.
* **M249 SAW:** Light machine gun utilized for high-volume fire infrastructure.
* **M203 Grenade Launcher:** Mounted directly under his rifle barrel.

## Close Combat & Special Gear
